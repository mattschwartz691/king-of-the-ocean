How to do Bidness

  • 1 · Bid. Every day 3 shops go up for auction. Secretly split your cash between them. Highest bid wins the shop and pays; the loser keeps their money.
  • 2 · Get paid. Each shop pays you every day. Shops earn +$1 for every neighbor you own (the fountain counts), so build next to yourself.
  • 3 · Lead the market. Own more of a shop type than your rival for a +$2 crown bonus. The day's hot trend pays +$2 per shop.
  • 4 · Beware worms! A lot nobody bids on becomes a worm hole (−$1 to its neighbors). On raid days, outbid your rival for the Early Bird or worms shut down your best shop.
  • 5 · Win. Richest slug after 8 days wins. The last payday counts double!
