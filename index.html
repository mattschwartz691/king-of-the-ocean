How to Play

  • Wear the crown. Your reign bar fills while you hold it. First to 45 seconds is King of the Ocean.
  • ⟫Saw Dash. Drag anywhere on the left to swim. Tap the right side to dash — hit a rival with your saw to stun them and knock the crown loose.
  • ◖Feeding frenzy. Slice through 6 fish for a burst of speed and rapid dashes. The golden fish fills it instantly.
  • ●Bubbles. Speed, Shield and Zap power-ups float around the reef.
  • ✺Jellyfish sting! Avoid them — or dash through to pop them.

Tip: on iPhone, tap Share → “Add to Home Screen” to play full-screen. Landscape feels best.
